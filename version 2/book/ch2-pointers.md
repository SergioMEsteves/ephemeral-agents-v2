# Chapter 2 — The Thesis (V2 pointer)

**Read the original: [`book/ch2-the-thesis.md`](../../book/ch2-the-thesis.md).**

The thesis and the six principles (P1–P6) carry forward unchanged.
What V2 adds is a test the V1 build could not run: **coexistence as a
testable prediction** — that the organization can live beside another
copy of itself on one estate with the principles and the gate
discipline held. Part II of the V2 book reports how that prediction
fared, including where it broke and what was changed.
