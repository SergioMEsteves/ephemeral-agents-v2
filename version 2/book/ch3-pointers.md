# Chapter 3 — The Organization (V2 pointer)

**Read the original: [`book/ch3-the-organization.md`](../../book/ch3-the-organization.md).**

The structure carries forward unchanged: three departments — business
judgment, engineering design, ephemeral execution — and the route of a
work order through them. What V2 changes is occupancy, not shape: the
same organization runs *twice* on one estate (V1 beside the `ea-`
build, Chapter 11), and the Chief of Staff — named here as a bus-org
principal — becomes a resident, gated part of the build (the CoS
milestone; see Specials 1 and 2).
